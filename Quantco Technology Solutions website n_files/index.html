ZapShare

Independent service

A Secure document handoff CYCLE

Your document. A controlled session. A defined purpose.

Unmanaged chats and ordinary Print vendor systems can leave copies outside your control. ZapShare explores short-lived, QR-based document transactions with Zero-Knowledge architecture for sensitive printing workflows.


Select document → Scan vendor QR →Controlled transfer → Print-only access → Online Payment method →Temporary data cleared → Receipt and limited metadata

Select a document on your device. Planned preparation includes encryption and user-approved print settings.

Scan an enrolled vendor’s QR code and confirm the intended terminal before approving a short-lived session.

A planned control plane pairs the devices. The intended secure transfer separates document content from signaling and audit records; persistent cloud document storage is not intended.

The vendor runtime and local print-agent concept limit routine access to printing. Supported printers, permissions, and runtime.

Planned cleanup targets temporary buffers. Operating-system spools, printer memory, physical copies, and external cameras remain residual risks After Payment confermation.

Limited metadata receipts support transaction accountability rather than document archiving. Retention, access, and consent policies will be defined.

Potential use cases in India: students, banking documents, government and land services, RTO workflows, SMEs, legal teams, and healthcare document handling etc.

Safety & Privacy / The problem

Digital trust requires more than a reassuring interface.

Suspicious links, manipulated media, unsafe document handling, and scams demand informed choices—and controls that make those choices practical.

Account security

Strong account protections and recovery controls require production validation.

Privacy controls

Review visibility, permissions, and access settings before sharing.

Blocking

Limit unwanted interactions with service-specific blocking controls when available.

Reporting

Reporting requires defined review procedures and response policies.

Suspicious content

Pause before reacting to urgent or emotionally charged content. Check the source.

Link safety

Check domains and avoid unexpected downloads. Appearance is not proof of safety.

Document safety

Verify the recipient and understand device, spool, and printer handling.

Verification awareness

Automated outputs support investigation; review underlying evidence.

Scam awareness

Treat urgency, impersonation, and requests for money or credentials cautiously.

Manipulated media

Images and video may be edited or miscaptioned. Examine context alongside visual clues.

Secure sharing

Share only what is necessary. Screenshots and recipient copies can exceed service controls.

User education

Clear explanations complement technical protection. No system removes every risk.

How ZapShare Works

Claims & limitations / Requires validation

Short-lived sessions and no persistent cloud document archive are intended. Encryption, vendor print-only access, supported-printer qualification, cleanup, and screenshot deterrence require implementation and testing. Spools, printer memory, physical copies, and external cameras create residual risks. No “nothing leaks,” zero-risk, or trace-free guarantee is asserted. Metadata needs defined retention and consent policies.
